LED Scroller – Privacy Policy

2026-10-18 · Android 2.1.0+

This policy applies to the native Android version 2.1.0 and later of LED Scroller – LED Banner (com.jasonj.ledscroller), provided by Jason / Next7.

Offline, without an account

The app works entirely on your device. No account or sign-in is needed. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, usage analytics or crash reports. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not request internet access.

Messages and settings stay on your device

Your banner text, colors, scrolling speed, size, direction, font, dot style, display mode and effect are stored locally so you can use them again. Banners you explicitly save as favorites are kept only in app-private storage on this device (up to 12); you can remove them individually in the app. When updating from the previous React Native version, the app may read its existing settings on the same device. No data is uploaded during this migration. You can replace your text and settings in the app or remove all app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app. The native app excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access. It keeps the screen awake only while a full-screen banner is displayed.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Google Play and contact

Google Play may process information independently under its own policies when distributing or updating the app. If you email support, the email provider processes your message and email address so we can respond. We do not receive banner messages from the app.

Changes

Future changes to the app’s privacy practices will be reflected on this page.

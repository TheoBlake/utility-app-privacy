LED Scroller – Privacy Policy

2026-10-18 · Android 2.2.0+

This policy applies to the native Android version 2.2.0 and later of LED Scroller – LED Banner (com.jasonj.ledscroller), provided by Jason / Next7.

Offline, without an account

The app works entirely on your device. No account or sign-in is needed. It does not collect, upload, sell or track personal data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, usage analytics or crash reports. Exporting or sharing happens only when you explicitly choose to save a file or send it to another app. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not request internet access.

Messages and settings stay on your device

Your banner text, colors, speed, size, direction, font, dot style, blink rates, borders, visual effects, orientation and presentation preferences are stored locally so you can use them again. Banners you explicitly save as favorites are kept only in app-private storage on this device (up to 12); you can remove them individually in the app. When updating from the previous React Native version, the app may read its existing settings on the same device. No data is uploaded during this migration. You can replace your text and settings in the app or remove all app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app. The native app excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Imported media and exports

If you choose an image, GIF, video or audio document in the Android system picker, the app reads only that selected document and copies it to private local storage. These files are not uploaded. Removing a background or audio selection clears it from your current sign; imported files can remain privately stored for saved signs until you clear app data or uninstall. The app applies size, type and decoding limits. Your device or document provider may have its own policies for the picker.

PNG and animated GIF exports are generated locally. When you select Save, Android asks you where to save the file. When you select Share, Android asks which app should receive a temporary read-only link to that exported file. That app or storage provider then handles the file under its own policies. No export is sent automatically.

Permissions and device features

The optional flashlight tool requests Android CAMERA permission only when you enable it, because Android uses that permission to control the torch. The app does not capture photos or videos. It requests no microphone, location, contacts, broad media or storage permission and does not request internet access. Music playback, flashlight and the optional screen-brightness override are limited to active foreground banner presentation and stop or restore when you leave or background it. A live wallpaper can display the visual sign after explicit Android confirmation; wallpaper never plays music or activates the flashlight. The screen stays awake only while a full-screen banner is displayed.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Google Play and contact

Google Play may process information independently under its own policies when distributing or updating the app. If you email support, the email provider processes your message and email address so we can respond. We do not receive banner messages, imported media or exports from the app.

Changes

Future changes to the app’s privacy practices will be reflected on this page.
